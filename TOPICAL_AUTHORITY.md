## Position and boundary

The site should help designers, fabricators, contractors, procurement teams, and buyers specify, inspect, store, machine, finish, and purchase wood-based panels. It owns plywood/multiplek terminology, veneer/core choices, panel construction, bond/exposure classes, faced panels, workmanship, applications, quality control, and evidence-based procurement.

It does not provide structural panel engineering, universal span/load tables, fire or emissions certification, chemical handling instructions, forestry legality declarations without documents, or location-swapped stock pages. Performance depends on the exact panel standard, lay-up, species, adhesive, grade, thickness, manufacturer, and exposure.

## Existing-corpus and internal-link rules

1. Existing product hubs (`/multiplek/`, `/triplek/*`) retain commercial intent. New articles support them without exact-match product slugs.
2. Product-plus-location pages must not seed new editorial pages; consolidate their repeated educational copy into MKY-01 through MKY-07.
3. MKY-01 is the selection hub and links to MKY-02 through MKY-07 according to the decision.
4. Every application article in MKY-13 links to performance/finish/fabrication owners instead of restating technical guidance.
5. MKY-12 owns all defect and acceptance intent; other topics link to it for inspection.
6. MKY-14 summarizes required evidence and links to each property owner; it does not infer quality from product names.
7. “Plywood,” “multiplek,” and “triplek” may overlap in local usage, so articles must state the construction being discussed rather than rely on the label alone.

## Evidence gates

- Species, grade, ply count, lay-up, dimensions, tolerances, density, moisture, bond class, strength, stiffness, fastener capacity, emissions, fire, biological durability, and formaldehyde/VOC claims require the named panel standard and traceable manufacturer/test data.
- “Marine,” “exterior,” “waterproof,” “phenolic,” “structural,” “food safe,” “low emission,” or “certified sustainable” must not be inferred from colour, branding, price, or generic product type.
- Forest legality, chain-of-custody, origin, and sustainability claims require current traceable documentation for the actual batch/supplier.
- Adhesive, coating, release agent, preservative, cleaner, and edge sealer guidance requires product technical/safety data and compatibility testing.
- Structural floors, roofs, walls, platforms, formwork, loads, spans, supports, and reuse cycles require project design, inspection, and applicable standards.
- Prices, stock, sheet yield, waste, lead times, service life, and warranties require dated, like-for-like evidence.
- Site photos, defect diagnoses, test results, and case studies require provenance and permission.
- Suspected delamination, structural damage, mould, chemical contamination, or unsafe loading requires isolation and competent assessment.

Publish MKY-01, MKY-02, MKY-03, MKY-05, MKY-12, and MKY-14 first. This creates a selection-to-acceptance path while performance and application pages await representative datasheets and batch documents.
